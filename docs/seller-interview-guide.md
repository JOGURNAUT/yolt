# Thrift seller interview guide

For 30–45 minute calls with Instagram thrift page owners. Goal: learn what they actually do today, not what they say they would use.

## Ground rules

- Ask about the **last time** something happened, not "usually" or "would you". Past behaviour is evidence; opinions about the future are not.
- Don't pitch the product until the last section. Once they know what you're building, answers turn polite.
- Ask to see things: DM threads (with buyer names hidden), the sold-tracking sheet, the UPI screen, shipping receipts.
- Record only with consent. Write down exact quotes, especially complaints and workarounds.
- Probe every pain point: "What did you do about it?" If they did nothing, it's probably not a real pain.

## 0. Opening (2 min)

- Explain: "We're researching how thrift pages run day to day. No right answers, and we're not selling anything."
- Ask for consent to take notes or record, and whether you can quote them anonymously.

## 1. The store (5 min)

1. How did the page start? How long has it been running?
2. Is this full-time, part-time or a side income? Who else helps (packing, photos, DMs)?
3. What do you sell: category, style niche, price range? What's your average selling price?
4. Roughly how many pieces do you sell in a normal week? In your best week?
5. Where else do you sell: WhatsApp, a website, pop-ups, flea markets, Vinted-style apps? What share comes from each?

## 2. Sourcing and prep (5 min)

6. Walk me through the last batch you sourced. Where from, how much did you buy, how did you pay?
7. How do you price a piece? Show me one recent example: cost, what you listed at, what it sold for.
8. What happens between buying and posting (washing, repairs, grading, photos)? How long does one piece take?
9. What share of what you source never sells? What do you do with it?

## 3. Posting and drops (7 min)

10. Walk me through your last drop: how you announced it, what time, how many pieces.
11. What happened in the first 10 minutes after posting? How many comments or DMs?
12. How do you decide who gets a piece when several people comment "BOOK"? Has anyone ever complained it was unfair?
13. How long do you wait for someone to pay before moving to the next person? How often does the first person ghost?
14. How do you keep track of what's booked, paid or sold? (Ask to see it.)
15. Have you ever sold the same piece twice? What happened?
16. Do you ever run auctions or bidding in comments? Why or why not?
17. Do buyers try to bargain? How do you handle it?

## 4. Payments (5 min)

18. How do buyers pay you? Personal UPI, business QR, payment link, COD?
19. How do you confirm a payment is real? Have you ever received a fake payment screenshot?
20. Why do or don't you offer COD? What happened the last time you did?
21. Have buyers refused to pay upfront because they didn't trust you? How often?
22. Do you have GST registration, a business bank account, or a registered business? Why or why not?
23. What fees do you pay today (payment gateway, shipping aggregator, anything else)?

## 5. Shipping (4 min)

24. Walk me through packing and shipping your last order. Which courier, and how do you book it?
25. What do you charge buyers for shipping, and what does it actually cost you?
26. How many days from payment to dispatch, typically? What delays it?
27. Do you ship out of state? What share of orders?
28. The last time a parcel was lost, delayed or damaged, what happened?

## 6. Returns, disputes and trust (5 min)

29. What's your return policy? Where do buyers see it?
30. Tell me about the last unhappy buyer. What was wrong (size, defect, not as pictured)? How was it resolved?
31. How do you show size and fit (tag size, measurements, model photos)? Do buyers still get it wrong?
32. How do new buyers decide you're not a scam? What do you do to prove it (reviews highlight, shipping videos)?
33. Have you been scammed as a seller (chargebacks, fake claims, buyers reselling)?

## 7. Instagram dependence (4 min)

34. Where do new buyers come from: explore, reels, hashtags, collabs, word of mouth, ads?
35. Has your reach dropped suddenly? When, and what did you do? What did it cost you in sales?
36. Do you pay for ads or influencer collabs? How much, and does it work?
37. If Instagram disappeared tomorrow, what would happen to your business?

## 8. Tools and time (3 min)

38. What tools do you use: Excel or Sheets, Shopify, Dukaan, WhatsApp Business, Canva, anything else?
39. In a typical week, where does most of your time go? What would you hand off first?
40. Have you tried a thrift or resale app or marketplace? Why did you stop, or why never?

## 9. Concept reaction (last 5 min, only now)

Describe briefly: a thrift marketplace where buyers filter by size and measurements, pay upfront with platform protection, and claim items in drops without DM chaos; sellers get an inventory tool and discovery beyond their followers.

41. What's your first reaction? What worries you?
42. What would make you list here as well as on Instagram? What would make you stop?
43. Would you keep stock in sync if a piece sells on Instagram first? How?
44. Which fee structure seems fair: commission per sale, a monthly fee, or per-listing? What rate would make you walk away?
45. Would you run a drop exclusively on the platform? What would you need to see first?
46. Would you allow size returns if the platform covered return shipping? If not, why not?

## Wrap-up (1 min)

- "Can we follow up? Can we watch your next drop live?"
- "Who else runs a thrift page we should talk to?"
- Offer something back: a summary of findings, or early access.
